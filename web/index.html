BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

A few good
questions.

Made for careful observation.
With room for uncertainty.

Can it recognise every plant and disease?

No. Species identification and disease checks use separate Pl@ntNet services with different coverage. Health results are candidates, and the app lets you inspect the current supported-condition list. The optional starter model covers only tomato, potato and bell pepper. A leaf alone cannot reliably reveal every cultivar.

Does installing it make scans work offline?

Online plant and disease identification still need an internet connection. Installation gives you an app icon and a separate app window on supported devices. Your saved journal and previously loaded guide can be browsed offline. The optional starter model works without internet only when the Leafwise server is running on your own computer.

Do I need an account or an API key?

Visitors do not need to create an account or provide a key. The website owner sets up the identification connection. Availability depends on that connection and its shared request allowance.

What happens to my photos and notes?

Photos are resized in your browser. Leafwise processes uploads in memory and does not save the originals on its server. With your permission, online scans send images without embedded location metadata to Pl@ntNet; its processing terms apply. Hosting services may keep connection logs. The journal, including saved thumbnails, stays in this browser. It does not sync across devices; export important notes before clearing browser data.

No analytics, advertising or location tracking are included. About the identification provider ↗

Where is the APK or App Store download?

Leafwise is an installable web app. Your browser handles installation; there is no APK or App Store package in this release. If your browser does not offer installation, you can still use the whole app on this website.
